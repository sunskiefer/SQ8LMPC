SQ8LMPC Panel Editor the KutilXXL editor, for the SQ8L panel

Drag a control to move it, drag the orange corner to resize it, or type exact numbers below. Every label is its own live text with its own x, y, w, h and text px. Nothing moves on its own. Export the layout, put layout.json in the repo and rebuild.
